Privacy Policy: Clear Solitaire

Effective date: 22 November 2026

Clear Solitaire: Big Cards ("the app", called "Clear Pasyans" in Turkish) is a solitaire game (Klondike, Spider, FreeCell) published on Google Play by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles and why. In short: we do not ask for your name, email or any account, and your games stay on your device. Two third-party services, Google AdMob (ads) and RevenueCat (the optional Pro purchase), receive limited data as described below.

1. Data stored only on your device

Your current games, statistics (games played and won, streaks, best times), daily challenge results and settings (card size, colours, language, theme) are saved locally on your phone. They are not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes them.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version may show ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.). Ads are never shown in the middle of a game. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • the device's IP address (which can be used to estimate its general location),
  • the Android advertising ID and app set ID,
  • ad interactions (for example ad views and taps) and app launch information,
  • diagnostic information about app and SDK performance.

This data is sent to Google, encrypted in transit. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings › Privacy options in the app. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads). Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy. Users who buy Pro see no ads.

3. The Pro purchase (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Pro is an optional one-time purchase, not a subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. To confirm and restore your purchase, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives your purchase history (purchase token, product, date and price), a random app user ID created by RevenueCat that is not linked to your name or email, and basic technical data such as device model, operating system, app version, country and IP address. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • The app does not require an account or sign-in.
  • The app asks for no special Android permissions; it uses only internet access (for ads and purchases) and Google Play billing.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Personalised advertising is based on your consent. Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and purchase validation rely on our legitimate interest in funding and running the app, and on carrying out the purchase you asked for.

6. Retention and deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete the app or its data. Data held by Google and RevenueCat is kept according to their policies. You can ask us to have the RevenueCat record tied to your purchase deleted, using the contact below; please include the approximate purchase date. After deletion, "Restore purchases" may no longer work.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we hold no data that identifies you, most requests are best sent to Google or RevenueCat, but we are glad to help.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/girayserter/clear-solitaire/issues, or use the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play page.
